Our Story

Friendship & love for the sport.

Founded in the heart of Stockholm, Bellevue United FC started as friends wanting to kick a ball without strict league commitments.

Today, we organize weekly friendly games where everyone is welcome. To keep the competitive spirit, we host small, action-packed daily tournaments three times a year.
